# Watering-Wonders
I first put an equal amount of dirt in 5 planter pots (P1-P5). I then put seeds in all the 5 pots. The seeds are the seeds that are shown in the list of materials. I next created a circuit using a Raspberry Pi. I then put 1 moisture sensor in each pot (MS1-MS5). I connected the moisture sensors to a breadboard that sends an input back to the Raspberry Pi when moisture is detected. When the Raspberry Pi knows that moisture is detected, it sends voltage to the corresponding numbered relay switch of the moisture sensor. Each channel on the relay switch is giving voltage to the corresponding numbered water motor of the relay switch channel (M1-M5). The water motors are put in a water tank and turn on when the moisture is detected, and a tube is put inside the corresponding pot of the moisture sensor, and then water is given to the plant. If it is P1 getting watered, it will only be watered for 1 second. If it is P2 getting watered, then it will be watered for only 2 seconds, etc. I then measured the growth of the plant in centimeters over the next month.
